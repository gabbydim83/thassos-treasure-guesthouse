Before you arrive

Good to know.

Can a child stay as a third guest in the room?

Yes, a third guest may be possible in a room, particularly for families with a child. Please include all guests in your request and specify how many are children. Bedding, room allocation, any additional charges and extra beach sunbeds are confirmed by the property.

How many guests can stay at Thassos Treasure?

There are eight private rooms, generally arranged for two guests each. A third guest, such as a child travelling with their family, may be accommodated in a room if confirmed by the host. We do not publish a fixed maximum group size for these flexible arrangements; please share your full guest count so we can check what is possible.

Does each room have its own kitchen?

No. Each room has its own private bathroom. There are two shared kitchens, one serving the four rooms on the ground floor and one serving the four rooms upstairs.

Are sunbeds included?

Beach access and a sunbed for every confirmed guest are included with the stay. If your family needs an additional sunbed because of an extra person in the room, mention this in your request so the host can confirm the arrangement.

How are Early Booking 2027 rates calculated?

The nightly rate is determined by the month in which the reservation is completed, not the month you travel. Full payment gives the lower nightly rate; alternatively a 35% deposit is due upon booking confirmation and 65% upon arrival, at the deposit-option rate.

Is my request an instant confirmed reservation?

No. The guesthouse checks dates and available rooms manually, then confirms the arrangements and final price. Payment is not collected through this preview website.
